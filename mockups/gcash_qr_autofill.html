GCash QR Detect & Zoom — payment_settings_page.dart / gcash_payment_screen.dart

Owner downloads the business GCash QR (name + number embedded) and uploads it once. The app crops/zooms the QR and decodes the EMVCo payload — the detected merchant name and number are shown on the payment screen only; Merchant Identity fields stay manual.
