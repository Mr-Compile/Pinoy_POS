Audit — before this change

Implemented: license_notice.dart (one widget, three tiers — LicenseNoticeTier.calm/warn/critical) mounted in app_shell.dart and at the top of login_screen.dart; license_status_screen.dart (owner page) reachable from Settings → System / Management → License. The signed activity log lives in the license blob (LicenseStatus.events).

1
No urgency escalation
Same amber at 30 days and 30 minutes. Proposal adds a critical tier (error tokens + alarm icon + "locks in Xh" copy) for the final ~48 hours.
2
Remaining time is mid-sentence
The Level-1 fact ("6 days") is buried in a full sentence. Proposal: bold title line carries the countdown; sub-line carries deadline + action hint.
3
Login renders it inside the card
Clipped by card radius, reads as part of the form. Proposal: same strip, page-top, consistent with the app shell.
4
"Enter code" tap target is ~36px
Shrink-wrapped TextButton. Proposal: 40px filled pill (warning/error solid) — easier to hit, clearly the primary action.
5
Developer contact only on the lock screen
Critical tier surfaces contactInfo inline so staff can act before the lock engages.
6
Two widgets, duplicated logic
Chip and banner share the same enter-code flow. Proposal: one LicenseNotice widget with a severity parameter (calm / warn / critical).
7
Deliberate: no dismiss button
Kept from the current design — the lock must never land silently, so the notice can't be swiped away.
8
Owner can't see license status
Today only the hidden dev panel shows deadline/state, and activity_logs can't hold license events (it requires a signed-in actor). Proposal: Settings → System → License tile (Owner) → status + enter-code + signed recent-activity log stored in the license blob.
